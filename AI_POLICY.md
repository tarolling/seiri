# AI Policy

We adopt [LLVM's AI policy](https://llvm.org/docs/AIToolPolicy.html). TLDR is that a human in the loop is required to make contributions.
No pull request should be open unless the code that is being merged has been reviewed by the
implementor themselves. Simple as that :)

[Here's an example of what NOT to do.](https://github.com/tarolling/seiri/pull/188)
